Team Sync Power Tools

Round one settled where Team Sync lives: a Thread view plus signals in the editor. This round answers three harder questions. How do people see what changed? What happens when two people change the same thing? And can CBCS give the full power of git without making it feel like git?

    Three layers of git

    Everything git can do for a small team is available, but it shows up only when someone reaches for it. Plain words come first. Git's names appear in tooltips and, if you turn it on, everywhere.

    Decisions · recorded 2 Nov 2026

    What was decided

    Decided · Reviewing incoming changes

    • B · Review tab, with a Show in file button

    Full editor space for reading, stays open while you look around, inline or side by side.

    Decided · Combining clashes

    • B · Combine tab

    Your files stay untouched until Finish, Cancel puts everything back, and several clashes stay manageable.

    Decided · History view

    • Plain list, with lanes only when experiments exist or advanced tools are on

    Reads simply for most shops without hiding the shape of the work when it matters.

    Decided · How much git to expose

    • Three layers, advanced off by default

    Beginners never trip over it; power users turn it all on in Settings › Team.

    Settled · Merge engine

    • CBCS writes its own three-way merge on Python's difflib

    Why: merge3 is GPL-2.0-or-later and CBCS is MIT, so bundling it would bring GPL obligations into the product. dulwich is dual-licensed and is used under Apache-2.0, with its notice shipped in the vendor bundle.
