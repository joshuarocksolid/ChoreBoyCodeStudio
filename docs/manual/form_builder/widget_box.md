# The widget box

The left pane header is `Insert`. This is the widget box.

## To add a field to a form

1. Click the canvas or the outline so the form has focus.
2. Press `/`, or click a tile in the Insert list.
3. Type a word such as `date`, `text`, or `list`.
4. Press Enter.

The new widget lands in the layout that has the selection. The undo name is `Insert` plus the friendly name, such as `Insert push button`.

You can also drag a tile onto the canvas.

`/` typed into a text field types a slash. Click the canvas first when you want the insert search.

## Names on the tiles

Each tile shows a plain name, such as `Line edit` or `Push button`. The Qt class, such as `QLineEdit` or `QPushButton`, is on the inspector once the widget is selected.

The groups, when they have something to show, are:

- Layouts and spacers
- Text and numbers
- Choices
- Buttons
- Dates and times
- Lists and tables
- Containers
- Display
- FreeCAD
- Your widgets

`Your widgets` lists classes this form already declares. `Recently used` sits above the groups. Saved pieces appear under `Saved pieces` after you save a piece.

Search can also offer a new form, such as `Main window · new form`. Enter on that row opens the New form tab. It does not drop a window onto the current page.

When nothing matches, the list says `Nothing called “…”` with your words in the quotes, then `Try text, list or date.`
